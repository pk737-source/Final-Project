Newark at a Glance

Newark sits along the Passaic River in northeastern New Jersey, about ten miles from midtown Manhattan. Founded in 1666, it grew from a small Puritan settlement into one of the most important industrial and transportation centers on the East Coast. Today it is New Jersey's largest city and home to a port, an international airport, major universities, and a wide mix of communities.

This site puts Newark's story in one place so you can look back and compare each era. You do not need a background in history or demographics to follow along.

  • Located in Essex County, New Jersey
  • Home to Port Newark and Newark Liberty International Airport
  • Shaped by waves of immigration and migration
